# Approved Master Blueprint v1.0
Approved November 25, 2026.
## Identity
Tiny Mobb: Turf Wars by CodePhrex Gaming. Small Characters. Big Egos. Dirty Politics. Fictional Viceborough; dark political satire; premium stylized miniature 3D designer figures. Fictional politicians, not real political endorsements. Android and Windows; offline campaign and online competitive play; 5–12 minute match target.
## Factions
Syndicate (gold, intimidation/reinforcements), Establishment (crimson, manipulation/taxation), Conglomerate (steel blue, wealth/acquisitions), Underground (burnt orange, speed/numbers), Anonymous (violet, deception/intelligence), Resistance (green, defense/counterattacks).
Founding boss concepts: Don Malice, Mayor Mayhem, Senator Sinister, Baron Billions, Lil' Havoc, Mr. Nobody, General Revolt.
## Combat
20 Authority per boss, 40-card decks, increasing Influence capacity, five districts: City Hall, Downtown, Financial District, Back Alley, Docks. Opposed units fight; unopposed units damage opposing boss. Zero Authority loses. District bonuses, corruption, bribery, scandal, propaganda, territory, loyalty, extortion, coalition, betrayal, public outrage. Shared Citywide Chaos meter (0–100), telegraphed citywide events including shutdown, market crash, uprising, blackout. Exact timing, targeting and numerical balance require specification and testing.
## Collection
Full-launch targets: 300 cards (120 common, 90 rare, 48 epic, 24 legendary, 12 mythical, 6 Black Label), 18 bosses, 60 missions. Black Label cosmetics confer no unfair advantage.
## Modes
Rise to Power campaign, Ranked Turf Wars, Underground Arena draft, Mobb Alliances, Training Grounds. Deck builder, collection, replays, achievements, customization, store, seasons and live events.
## Visual identity
Obsidian #08090D, gunmetal #20232B, gold #B89A60, crimson #8B182B, violet #553780, ivory #E8E3D9. Oversized expressive heads, miniature bodies, premium detailed clothing, cinematic noir lighting. Approved concept art is the visual authority; do not substitute generic assets.
## Commercial
Free base game, cosmetic-first monetization, earnable cards, optional currency and direct purchases. Proposed season pass $9.99, skins $1.99–14.99, headquarters $4.99–19.99. No pay-to-win. Paid randomized rewards require separate review. Trademark and rating clearance pending.
## Architecture
Godot 4, GDScript, optimized 2D with 3D effects, Blender assets, Nakama, PostgreSQL, local offline storage, tactical AI, server-authoritative multiplayer, GitHub Actions, Android and Windows installers.
